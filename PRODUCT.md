# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Listeners use wocli-music while working in a terminal, switching between music, development, and other desktop tasks. They navigate by keyboard or mouse and may glance at lyrics without opening the player.

## Product Purpose

Provide a lightweight native music player that makes search, playback, library management, lyrics, and music controls available through a terminal, command line, and desktop companion.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, practical, lightweight. Music and controls should be clear without distracting from the user's work.

## Anti-references

Avoid advertising-heavy music interfaces, decorative clutter, intrusive windows, and unnecessary runtime or setup requirements. Avoid covering system controls or silently obscuring text with playback buttons.

## Design Principles

- Keep listening and playback actions easy to find and use.
- Put readable lyrics and current playback state before decoration.
- Adapt to constrained terminal and taskbar space without hiding essential controls or overlapping system UI.
- Preserve familiar keyboard, mouse, and platform conventions.
- Keep interfaces responsive and respect the user's active work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Unicode and Chinese text, keyboard and mouse interaction, narrow terminal windows, and display scaling. Preserve legibility and usable controls across system appearances without assuming a single display configuration.
